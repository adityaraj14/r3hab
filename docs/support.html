What R3hab does

R3hab is a personal, offline-first rehab log for jumper’s knee (patellar tendinopathy) and quadratus lumborum (QL) strain. You record daily pain check-ins and training sessions, then resolve each session 24 hours later — Better, Same, or Worse — to decide whether to stay, soft cut, progress, or hard drop your load. Charts show the 7- and 28-day trend.

Everything is stored on your device. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Common questions

How do I back up my data? Settings → Export JSON backup. Save the file somewhere you control (Files, iCloud Drive, etc.). Import later with Replace or Merge.

Steps aren’t filling in. Open the iOS Health app → Sharing → Apps → R3hab and make sure Steps is allowed. R3hab only reads step count and never writes to Health.

Reminders aren’t showing. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → R3hab, and confirm reminders are turned on in the app’s Settings.

How do I start over? Settings → Clear all log entries removes check-ins and sessions but keeps your phase and settings. Deleting the app removes everything.

Not medical advice. R3hab is a logging tool, not a medical device, and nothing in the app is a diagnosis or treatment plan. See a clinician for a diagnosis, and seek care promptly for red flags such as sudden severe pain, swelling or a hot joint, inability to bear weight, numbness or weakness, fever, or pain that keeps getting worse despite rest.
