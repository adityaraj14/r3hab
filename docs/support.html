R3hab for iOS

What R3hab is

R3hab is a personal knee rehab diary for jumper’s knee (patellar tendinopathy). You log daily pain check-ins and training sessions, then resolve each session 24 hours later to decide how to adjust your load.

Everything is stored on your device. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

Found a bug or have a question? A sentence on what happened, plus your iOS version, is enough.

Email support

Not medical advice. R3hab is a logging tool, not a medical device. Nothing in the app is a diagnosis or treatment plan — see a clinician for that, and seek care promptly if pain is severe, changes suddenly, or keeps getting worse despite rest.
